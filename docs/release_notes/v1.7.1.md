# UniPlaySong v1.7.1

**Released: 2026-08-08**
**Requires Playnite 10.52+ (API 6.16.0)**

**Highlight:** A **Quick Start** tab that sets UniPlaySong up in one click — for anyone who wants the basics working without reading through every settings tab.

## Added

- **Quick Start — set up UniPlaySong in one click.** Five ways of listening per mode, applied in one click. Pick **Hover Preview** (music follows the highlight, as a looping 30-second clip or the full track), **Select to Play** (Fullscreen — a game's music starts when you open it), **Background Mode** (Desktop — one ambient track, uninterrupted), **Radio Mode** (one continuous mix from your library or Spotify), or **Huddini Showcase** (everything on: crossfades, live effects, volume boost).

  Applying an option only changes the playback settings it covers — your volume, tool paths, pause rules and effects are left alone — and **Undo** or **Reset to my settings** puts things back.

- **A Setup tab.** yt-dlp and FFmpeg now live on one page, so there's a single stop before anything else works.

- **A Hidden achievement sound.** Playnite Achievements can fire a separate sound for hidden/secret unlocks, sitting between Ultra-Rare and Capstone. A sound is bundled for it, and you can swap in your own.

## For theme developers

- **Hidden achievement unlocks.** Add `hidden.[audio extension]` to your theme's `audio/Achievements/` folder to give secret unlocks their own sound. `.mp3`, `.ogg`, `.wav` and `.flac` all work — first match wins. Playnite Achievements sends this one as `playnite://uniplaysong/playniteachievements/hidden`; note it has no `achievement` suffix, unlike the other five tiers.

## Improved

- **Settings are easier to get through.** Long tabs now group into collapsible sections instead of one long scroll, options sit on the tab they belong to, and the tab order starts with About and Setup. Several sections were renamed to say what they actually do — "Achievement Unlock Sounds" is now "PlayniteAchievements Unlocks", "Window State" is now "Window Focus", and the completion/abandoned sections now name the game status they follow.

- **Smoother library navigation in Fullscreen.** Preparing the next song no longer happens on the thread that draws the screen, so browsing doesn't hitch while a track loads. The fade timers also no longer take priority over drawing and controller input.

- **Much quieter logging.** With debug logging off, UniPlaySong writes only errors to Playnite's shared log instead of hundreds of routine lines. Turning **Enable Debug Logging** on restores the full detail.

- **A tidier About tab.** Known issues that no longer apply are gone, the Active Theme Music and autosuspend notes are shorter, and there's a Thanks section — Mike Aniki for the ANIKI REMAKE integration, Justin Delano for the PlayniteAchievements integration, and everyone who reported a bug.

## Fixed

- **Switching audio engine no longer leaves the old one running.** Turning Live Effects, the Visualizer or Crossfade on or off — or opening a game with retro chiptune music, which switches engines automatically — left the previous engine alive in the background, doing work for the rest of the session.

- **Reset on the Experimental tab now covers everything on it.** Media key control and the two fade curve settings were skipped.

## Previous Version (v1.7.0)

- Radio Mode can play through your gaming sessions; "Play music only on game select" now works with Radio Mode; faster shutdown; taskbar media buttons control Spotify when it's your radio source.
