# UniPlaySong v1.7.2

## ControlUp support

If you use the [ControlUp](https://github.com/aHuddini/ControlUp) plugin, UniPlaySong can now
play a sound the moment it detects your controller — on connect or on the hotkey.

Set it up in **Settings → Gamification → ControlUp Events**. Use the bundled "Coin Pickup" or
point it at your own audio file, with Preview on both. It's off by default, so turn it on there
and in ControlUp.

## Audio Buffer Size (Experimental)

**Settings → Experimental → Audio Buffer Size** controls how much audio UniPlaySong prepares
ahead of the speakers. A smaller buffer starts sounds sooner — most noticeable on short
notification sounds — while a larger one is safer against crackling on a busy machine.

The default is unchanged, so nothing moves unless you go looking for it. Takes effect after
restarting Playnite.

## Also fixed

Notification sounds no longer pay a startup delay on every play for users who had only the
achievement or ControlUp sound enabled. The audio device was only being prepared in advance when
a completion or abandoned jingle was also switched on.

---

**Requires Playnite 6.16.0 or newer.**
