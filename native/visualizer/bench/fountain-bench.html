Help

Hold C to see the pinned values (pinned when the page loads, or with Pin as compare); let go to come back. Graphs: one column's level, kick and fling over the last 3 s - pick the column by hovering over the line. Drop an audio file anywhere. Click a parameter's name to reset it. Values are remembered in this browser.

Beat lane: the steady pulse you'd tap your foot to, found the way librosa (a standard music-analysis library) does it - the easiest thing to check: pick "Ticks: the beat" and hear whether the metronome stays with the song. Drum lanes (with a song or the drum test playing): the hits found for kick, snare, hats and bass notes, 1.5 s either side of now. On the drum test, white outlines are the real hits. Ticks: kick low, snare mid, hats high, bass notes lowest. Found live, a kick is known ~110 ms after it lands (the kick-or-bass check), a snare ~40 ms, a hat ~25 ms.
